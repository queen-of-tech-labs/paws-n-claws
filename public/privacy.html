Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Paws & Claws Pet Companion ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and, if you use Google Sign-In, your name and profile information from Google.
  • Pet Information: Details you provide about your pets, including health logs, medications, and reminders you create.
  • Location Information: When you search for nearby veterinarians or animal rescues, we process the ZIP code or address you enter to return relevant results. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Payment Information: If you subscribe to a premium tier, payment is processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your credit card details ourselves.
  • Notification Data: If you enable push notifications, we use OneSignal to deliver reminders and updates to your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain core app features (pet tracking, reminders, lost pet flyers)
  • To process premium subscription payments
  • To send you notifications you've opted into
  • To improve app functionality and fix issues

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Google Firebase (authentication and data storage)
  • Google Maps Platform (location search for vets/rescues)
  • Stripe (payment processing)
  • OneSignal (push notifications)

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase/Firestore. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
